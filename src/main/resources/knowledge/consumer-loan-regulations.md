# Sample Consumer Loan Regulations

MOCK DOCUMENT. Sample internal documentation of a fictional bank prepared for the credit-assistant demo. It does not describe any real product and is not financial advice.

## Loan amount and repayment period
The sample consumer loan is granted for an amount between 5000 and 150000. The repayment period is between 6 and 96 months, chosen by the customer when the agreement is signed.

## Installments
The sample consumer loan is repaid in equal monthly installments that include principal and interest. The annual interest rate is fixed for the whole repayment period.

## Payment due date
Each monthly installment is due on the 15th day of the month. If the 15th day falls on a weekend or public holiday, the installment is due on the next business day. An installment that is not paid in full by its due date becomes overdue.
