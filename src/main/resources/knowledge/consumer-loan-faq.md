# Sample Consumer Loan FAQ

MOCK DOCUMENT. Sample internal documentation of a fictional bank prepared for the credit-assistant demo. It does not describe any real product and is not financial advice.

## Can the customer change the payment due date?
Yes. The customer may change the monthly payment due date once per calendar year, to the 5th, 15th or 25th day of the month. The change is free of charge and must be requested at least 10 days before the next installment due date.

## Which documents are required to apply?
The customer must present a valid identity document and a statement of monthly income covering the last 3 months.

## Is loan insurance covered by this documentation?
No. Loan insurance is not covered by the sample consumer loan documentation. Advisors must not provide information about insurance for this product based on this knowledge base.
