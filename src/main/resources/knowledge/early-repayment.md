# Early Repayment of the Sample Consumer Loan

MOCK DOCUMENT. Sample internal documentation of a fictional bank prepared for the credit-assistant demo. It does not describe any real product and is not financial advice.

## Full early repayment
The customer may repay the whole outstanding principal of the sample consumer loan at any time before the end of the loan agreement. The customer must submit an early repayment request at least 5 business days before the planned repayment date. After full early repayment the loan agreement ends.

## Partial early repayment
The customer may repay part of the outstanding principal at any time before the end of the loan agreement. The minimum partial early repayment amount is 1000. After a partial early repayment the customer chooses whether to shorten the repayment period or to reduce the monthly installment.

## Early repayment fees
The sample consumer loan charges no additional fee or commission for full or partial early repayment. When repaying early, the customer pays only the outstanding principal and the interest accrued up to the repayment date.
